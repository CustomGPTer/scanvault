Only scan, sign, alter or share documents you are entitled to. Signing a document with ScanVault does not by itself make a signature legally valid for every purpose. Blacking out and Smart Erase change pictures; they are not a way to alter official documents, and you must not use them to mislead anyone.

Text, counts, positions and photos

Text reading, translation, counting, positions, grid references and photo sizing are worked out automatically and can be wrong. Check anything that matters. CountCam's totals are estimates to be checked by eye. Grid references come from the phone's GPS and are only as accurate as it is. Passport and ID photos are made to published sizes, but whether a photo is accepted is decided by the office that checks it.

Timestamp photos and checks

A timestamp photo shows the phone's clock and position at the time it was taken. Check a photo can only recognise photos taken on the same phone, and only confirms that a file is byte for byte the one recorded. It is a helpful record, not a certified evidence service.
