What ScanVault does not do

ScanVault is not on Google Play, so you install it from this site. It has no cloud sync or sharing between your devices, and no conversion to Word, Excel or PowerPoint: text comes out as plain text.

Where the others do more

CamScanner adds cloud sync across devices and team collaboration. Adobe Scan saves to Adobe Document Cloud and works with Acrobat Reader. Both are on Google Play.
