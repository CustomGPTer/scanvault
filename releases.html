Release notes

What changed in each version of ScanVault. The newest is at the top.

Version 1.0 22 November 2026Latest

  • First release. A document scanner that keeps every page encrypted on your phone, with no account, no cloud and no adverts.
  • Scanning: automatic edge finding, auto-capture when the page is still, batch scanning that waits for the next page, a magnifier crop and eight looks with a strength slider.
  • Camera modes: Document, ID card (front and back on one A4 page at real size, with black-out and a watermark), Book (two pages per photo, split at the spine), Whiteboard, Slides and QR codes.
  • Imports: photos, HEIC pictures and PDFs, from the app or shared in from other apps.
  • Text: every page read on the phone, search inside scans, copy and share text, and offline translation with downloadable language packs.
  • Sharing: searchable PDFs, JPG, PNG or one long image, four compression levels, page numbers, a watermark and AES-256 password protection. Printing.
  • Documents: folders, tags, favourites, merge, extract and split pages, reorder, and a recycle bin.
  • Sign and mark up: drawn, typed or photographed signatures, text, ticks, crosses, dates, twelve rubber stamps and your own, pen, highlighter, shapes and white-out.
  • Black out: drag boxes or find words, burned in for good when you save.
  • Smart Erase: paper fill, AI fill on the phone, and one-tap removal of blue, red or green pen.
  • Timestamp camera with date, time, OS grid reference, position, project, name, notes and logo, each photo with a reference and a fingerprint, plus Check a photo.
  • CountCam for round and square ends, with tap to add or remove.
  • Passport and ID photos in 45 sizes, with background swap and a 6×4 print sheet.
  • App lock and ID Vault (both off until you switch them on), password-sealed backup and restore, a home-screen widget, a Quick Settings tile and app shortcuts.
  • Help and tips, More apps, and a once-a-day update check that can be switched off.

Checking your download

The file for version 1.0 is called ScanVault.apk. It is 66.3 MB (69,545,999 bytes). Its SHA-256 checksum is:

5160aa9697cffc13bdc4e0c67b63e87f283e1059460908ed8d283d3815440d53

On Windows, run certutil -hashfile ScanVault.apk SHA256 and compare. Every version is signed with the same key, so each one installs over the last and keeps what you have saved.

Older versions are on the GitHub releases page.
